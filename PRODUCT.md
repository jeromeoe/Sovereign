# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Sovereign is for university students who want AI to behave like a persistent
private tutor rather than a blank chatbot. The initial experience prioritizes
students who are new to AI-assisted study, while progressively revealing
controls for experienced users who want to direct sessions more precisely.

Students usually arrive with a course, lecture material, and limited time. Their
primary job is to understand one concept, practise it under useful constraints,
and leave knowing what to study next.

## Product Purpose

Sovereign turns ordinary AI access into a guided learning system. It conducts
focused tutoring conversations, grounds explanations in the student's own
materials, and can place relevant slide diagrams directly beside the teaching
that refers to them.

Sessions are intentionally temporary. Before a transcript expires, Sovereign
distils durable learning evidence: concepts studied, time spent, demonstrated
mastery, recurring misconceptions, and future review priorities. The transcript
is then removed so students begin the next session with a clean context and do
not waste model credits on indefinitely growing history.

Success means a student can begin without prompt-engineering knowledge, trust
Sovereign's recommended next action, understand why it was recommended, and
return to a tutor that remembers their learning without retaining every word
they have said.

## Brand Personality

Calm, formidable, lucid.

Sovereign should feel intellectually serious, quietly capable, and composed
under pressure. Its confidence comes from clear reasoning and precise guidance,
not grandiosity, ornament, or motivational noise.

## Anti-references

- Childish or mascot-led education products that trivialize difficult study.
- Medieval, heraldic, royal, or luxury-banking interpretations of “Sovereign.”
- Neon AI dashboards, decorative glass surfaces, and conspicuous futuristic
  effects.
- Flashy or ornamental animation that competes with reading and thinking.
- Endless chat archives that reward remaining in one thread instead of learning.
- Dense expert controls exposed before a beginner understands why they matter.

## Design Principles

1. **Recommend before configuring.** Give beginners a strong next action, then
   reveal control as their intent becomes more specific.
2. **Teach with evidence in reach.** Keep the conversation central while making
   the exact slide, diagram, or learner-history evidence available at the moment
   it becomes useful.
3. **Retain the learning, not the conversation.** Temporary dialogue should
   produce durable, inspectable knowledge about the student's progress.
4. **Protect attention and credits.** Session limits, context warnings, and
   breaks should help students reset deliberately rather than punish them.
5. **Make intelligence feel quiet.** Hierarchy, typography, and interaction
   quality should carry the experience; decoration should never simulate depth.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support complete keyboard navigation, visible focus states,
screen-reader-friendly structure, color-blind-safe semantic states, readable
contrast, reduced-motion preferences, and comfortable text scaling. Use plain
language for beginner-facing controls without lowering the intellectual ceiling
of the tutoring itself.
